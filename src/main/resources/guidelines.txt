Official Blood Donation Eligibility Guidelines:
1. AGE REQUIREMENT: The donor must be between 18 and 65 years old.
2. WEIGHT REQUIREMENT: The donor must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).
3. DONATION FREQUENCY: There must be a minimum of 90 days (3 months) between consecutive whole-blood donations.
4. TATTOO AND PIERCING: The donor must wait for 6 months (180 days) after getting a tattoo, body piercing, or acupuncture treatment before donating.
5. TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS: Donors who have traveled to malaria-endemic regions must wait for 12 months after returning.
6. MEDICAL CONDITIONS: Donors must not have an active fever, flu, cold, sore throat, or infection on the day of donation.
7. MEDICATION: Donors currently taking antibiotics must wait for 7 days after completing their full course of treatment.
8. PREGNANCY AND BREASTFEEDING: Pregnant women or breastfeeding mothers are not eligible to donate blood. Wait at least 6 months after delivery.
9. PRE-DONATION CARE: Get at least 6-8 hours of sleep the night before, eat a healthy meal, and drink plenty of fluids. Do not consume alcohol within 24 hours before donating.
10. POST-DONATION CARE: Rest for 10 to 15 minutes at the donation site, drink plenty of water, keep the bandage on for a few hours, and avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exercise for 24 hours.
11. HEALTH FACTOR: Hemoglobin levels must be at least 12.5 g/dL. Blood pressure should be between 90/60 mmHg and 180/100 mmHg.
